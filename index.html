About APEX

What's under the hood.

APEX is built on raw TrackMan pitch-tracking data from the 2026 NCAA Division I season. Every pitch is classified, aggregated by arm, and turned into arsenal profiles, pitch-shape metrics, location heatmaps and similarity comps.

Pitch classification uses TrackMan's human tags with an auto-tag fallback, normalized into standard buckets (4-Seam, Sinker, Cutter, Slider, Sweeper, Curveball, Changeup, Splitter). Handedness is inferred from release side. Horizontal break is shown arm-side-positive.

Metrics — velocity, spin, induced vertical break (iVB), horizontal break (HB), extension, release height/side, vertical approach angle (VAA) and whiff rate — are pitch-type averages over the season.
