About APEX

What's under the hood.

APEX runs on raw TrackMan pitch tracking from the 2026 NCAA Division I season — every pitch classified, aggregated by arm, and turned into arsenal profiles, models, MLB comps, heatmaps and outing reports.

Stuff+ & Command+ are calibrated against FanGraphs' 2025 MLB Stuff+ (154 arms) and normalized so 100 = D1 average, ±10 = one SD. Sliders, four-seams, changeups and sinkers validate well (within-type r ≈ 0.43–0.61); curveball stuff is the hardest to reproduce from public data (FanGraphs uses proprietary spin-axis inputs), and sweepers are scored with the validated slider model.

MLB comps match each pitch to the nearest 2025 Statcast MLB pitch of the same type and handedness by velocity and movement.
